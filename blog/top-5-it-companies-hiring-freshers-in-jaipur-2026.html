How freshers can prepare for software roles

  1. Pick one track: Java, Python, full-stack development, QA, data or cloud.
  2. Build two complete projects with clear GitHub documentation, screenshots and a concise explanation of your contributions.
  3. Practise SQL, Git, debugging, REST APIs, data structures and communication skills relevant to the role.
  4. Use each company's official website to check for internships, junior roles or project opportunities; do not assume that recruitment is open.
  5. Tailor your resume to actual job descriptions and never pay an unverified recruitment fee.
