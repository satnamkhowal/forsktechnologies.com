How to enquire

  1. Review the service area relevant to your interests.
  2. Review the contact page and confirm a working communication channel before sharing personal information. The standalone template forms may download text locally rather than send an enquiry.
  3. Ask the team to confirm whether opportunities exist, along with any eligibility, supervision, duration, location and fees. Request written details of any assessment, certificate criteria, payment or refund terms before making a commitment.

For a useful initial enquiry, mention the technology area you want to explore, your current skills, preferred dates and whether you seek a learning project or a formal employment internship. Ask for the opportunity type and any written terms before sharing a résumé, identity documents or payment details.

Review contact information

Privacy reminder: For an initial enquiry, avoid sending identity scans, bank account details, passwords or payment credentials. Verify the recipient and the purpose of any requested documents through an official channel before sharing them.

Questions to ask before proceeding

  • Is this a current opening, a learning project, or only an enquiry about future opportunities?
  • What are the expected hours, location or remote arrangements, supervision, and any eligibility requirements?
  • Are there any fees, stipends, certificates, or assessments? Ask for written terms rather than assuming these are included.

How to confirm your enquiry was received

Downloading a text file from a template form does not submit your enquiry to Forsk Technologies. Do not treat a downloaded file or an on-screen message as confirmation that the team received it. Use a separately verified contact channel and request an acknowledgement before assuming your enquiry has reached anyone.

Important clarification

This page is an information and enquiry route, not an offer of employment or a confirmed internship program. No stipend, placement, certification, project allocation or acceptance is guaranteed.
